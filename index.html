01 · My Real Story & Work

Learned from the Billing Counter Upward. Built on Daily Store Discipline.

I started my working life right after my Class XII board exams in April 2021 at the billing counter of Mir Electronics, handling POS invoices, cash closing, Goods Received Note (GRN) logs, and shelf stock checks before moving to Amritsar in July 2021 for my Bachelor of Business Administration (BBA).

During my final semester of BBA in 2024, I worked as a BFSI Operations & Sales Intern at Bajaj Allianz General Insurance in Amritsar, where I assessed customer needs, explained insurance options, assisted with KYC and policy paperwork, and received Star Performer recognition. Alongside that period, I completed the HR Workshop under the Certificate Programme in Banking, Finance and Insurance conducted by Bajaj Finserv Limited in association with Amritsar Group of Colleges (Certificate of Completion, Credential ID: BFS350193).

After completing my BBA in June 2024, I returned to Mir Electronics in July 2024 as Store Manager (Lead Operations). Most of my week goes into making sure the showroom runs cleanly every day: managing floor sales, planning shifts for a team of more than 10 staff, checking physical stock against system records, and handling customer issues.

Two pieces of work at the store stand out. Alongside my regular showroom work, I also guide visiting retail shop owners on basic stock planning, ledger clean-up, and store setup under a profit-sharing arrangement with company ownership. 04 · Commercial Control & B2B Advisory

How We Recovered ₹80 Lakh in Overdue Customer Payments Over 15 Months.

Recovering old pending dues in a regional retail market requires patience, accurate records, and steady follow-up without damaging long-term customer relationships. Here is the exact 4-step process I ran at Mir Electronics, which I now also share with visiting shop owners under our profit-sharing advisory arrangement:

Step 01 · Ledger Audit

Reconcile Historical Entries

Went through older customer account books line by line, matching past bills, part-payments, and Goods Received / return notes to remove disputed entries.

Step 02 · Balance Confirmation

Confirm the Exact Figure

Met or spoke with each account holder directly to review the corrected ledger statement so both sides agreed on the exact outstanding balance.

Step 03 · Realistic Schedules

Agree Fixed Installments

Structured practical weekly or monthly repayment plans based on the customer's cash flow rather than demanding impossible lump sums.

Step 04 · Weekly Discipline

Fixed-Day Follow-Up & Receipting

Followed up on a fixed day every week and recorded every installment immediately into the store ledger, recovering ₹80 lakh over 15 months.
